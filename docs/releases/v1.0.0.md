Ultimit 1.0. Everything from the 0.1 releases is in, plus new TASKS, DISKS, ITEMS and EMBER apps, NekoMail and NekoPlayer built in, your own accent colour, smoother animations and a stack of crash fixes.

**Updating:** Settings › Updates › Ultimit offers it as usual. Edge PCs (`ultimit-update --edge`) already run all of this.

### New apps

- **TASKS, a task manager.** Ctrl+Alt+Delete opens it, and it stays above fullscreen games. Processes with sort, search and Apps/Mine/All filters, End, Kill and Pause, and a performance tab with CPU, memory, GPU, disk and network graphs.
- **DISKS.** Every drive with a partition bar and its health (SMART/NVMe). Mount, eject, format, create, resize and delete partitions, with progress while it works. The drive the system runs from is refused. A **usage** view shows what takes space and opens it in DATA.
- **ITEMS, device settings.** For mice and keyboards that keep their own settings. First up: the Redragon M990 Legend (lighting, DPI levels, every button).
- **EMBER, a desktop assistant you can plug any AI into.** Claude or anything OpenAI-compatible (OpenAI, Grok, Ollama, LM Studio, llama.cpp). She runs commands, works with files, apps, windows, the web and the clipboard, and asks before anything risky. She's a bit of a brat about it.
- **NekoMail** is built in and handles `mailto:` links. **NekoPlayer** is the music player.

### The desktop

- **Your own accent colour** on any skin (Settings › Appearance): eight presets or a hex value. It colours the focused window's frame, selections, toggles and the cursor.
- **Smoother animations** at your monitor's refresh rate. Maximize, snap and restore now morph the window into place, and panels ease open and closed.
- **Photo frames on the desktop.** Drag them around, resize or lock them, from the desktop menu, DATA or IMAGE.
- **IMAGE opens as a picture library** when started on its own: thumbnails, folders and Places.
- **New mouse cursors:** Izy (white darts) is the default, with Shard still in Settings, and both come in more sizes.
- **Claude in the corner:** a small chat above the tray that expands into the full CLAUDE window. CLAUDE also answers Claude's multiple-choice questions in the window, and uses smaller models for grunt work.
- **WWW and CONSOLE** on the rail, for the browser and a terminal.
- Resize windows from the top edge and corners. Drag the top or bottom edge to the screen edge to stretch a window full height.
- **Your own snap widths**, with an editor in Settings.
- The wallpaper and the ticker stop while a game is fullscreen on any screen, and the desktop clock only ticks each minute.
- GTK window buttons match Ultimit's title bar, and Flatpak apps follow a skin change straight away.
- USB disks mount by themselves. Ultimit asks before mounting a Windows drive and can read dynamic disks.

### Games

- Proton games on NVIDIA use NVIDIA's newest DLSS. **AION 2** works, and so do **MapleStory** and **MapleStory Worlds**.
- Fullscreen games pick the right monitor on PCs with a portrait side screen.
- Steam's dropdown menus work: clicking one no longer closes it.

### Fixes

- A crash after waking from sleep no longer comes back unlocked: the desktop restarts on the lock screen.
- No crash when a monitor disappears on wake, and none at logout.
- Edge updates no longer hang at "Trimming Ubuntu's server parts".
- The disk automounter quits with the session instead of piling up.
- fastfetch shows the real installed version.
